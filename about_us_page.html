About Us

Dedicated to strengthening communities and promoting good change in Gaza and beyond. Since the day we were founded, we've never wavered in our commitment to giving people the support they need to build resilience.

Our Mission

Our mission at HFG Charity is firmly anchored in the transformative power of compassion and community. We're driven by the desire to give families and individuals the resources and tools they need to succeed. Using a comprehensive strategy, we aim to address not just short-term needs but also long-term challenges, so the change we make lasts.

Our Vision

We have a bold but attainable vision: a world in which every person, regardless of circumstance, has the chance to thrive. Our goal is to help build resilient, self-sufficient communities that can withstand hardship, access quality healthcare, and receive essential education — contributing to a global society where everyone's wellbeing is valued.

Our Approach

We believe in community-driven solutions and close collaboration. Working with regional partners and stakeholders lets us pinpoint real needs, craft tailored solutions, and see them through to successful delivery. Our programmes cover a range of projects, including:

  • Educational support and scholarships
  • Community development projects
  • Healthcare and medical assistance

Our Impact

We've seen the transformative effect of our work first-hand over the years. Every success story — from saving lives through medical care to helping students reach their academic goals — reflects our commitment to lasting change. With the combined efforts of our staff, volunteers, sponsors and partners, we keep helping people and communities overcome obstacles and build better futures.

Join Us

We believe everyone can contribute to positive change. Your contribution counts, whether you give resources, give your time, or simply help raise awareness. Working together, we can meaningfully improve the lives of those in need and build a more caring, inclusive society. Thank you for being part of our pursuit of empowerment, resilience and hope.
