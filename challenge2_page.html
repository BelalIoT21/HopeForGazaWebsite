Threads of Unity: Gaza Solidarity Bracelets

Wear your support, one bead at a time.

Our Gaza Solidarity Bracelets are more than accessories — they're a symbol of resilience, unity, and hope. Here's how you can take part in the movement:

Handcrafted beauty. Using beads in the colours of the Palestinian flag — red, black, white and green — local artisans carefully thread each bracelet together to create a striking piece of jewellery that embodies solidarity with Gaza.

Wear it with pride. A Gaza Solidarity Bracelet is more than a fashion statement — it's a visible show of support for the people of Gaza, and a starting point for conversations about the challenges Gazans face and why supporting their cause matters.

Raise awareness. Let people know what your bracelet means. Help others understand the daily struggles and hardships Gazans face, including the effects of the conflict and the ongoing humanitarian crisis. By drawing attention to the issue, you help advocate for change and give a voice to those in need.

Support the cause. Proceeds from every bracelet sold go directly toward our relief work in Gaza — helping provide access to clean water, basic healthcare, and educational opportunities. Every bracelet purchased helps fund the initiatives that improve living conditions and build hope for a better future.

Why it matters

These bracelets are a reminder that even small acts of kindness can lead to big change. By wearing one, you show your support for peace, justice, and a better future for Gaza. Each bead represents a step toward resilience, community, and optimism for people in need. Together, we can build a network of support that crosses borders and brings us closer to a world where everyone can thrive. Visit our website or one of our local events to get your bracelet, and join us as we weave a fabric of empathy and solidarity for the people of Gaza.
