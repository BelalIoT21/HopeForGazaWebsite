Lifeline for Gaza's Health

Healing hands, saving lives — medical aid.

Gaza's healthcare system is on the brink, struggling with limited resources against overwhelming demand. In response, our medical aid programme is a source of hope and support for Gaza's most vulnerable people:

Essential medicines. We supply clinics and hospitals with vital medications and medical disposables, ensuring life-saving treatments are readily available to those who need them — from insulin to antibiotics, every medication offered has the potential to relieve suffering and restore health.

Financial support. We provide essential financial assistance for medical treatments, surgeries, and emergency care — whether that's covering the cost of a life-saving operation or funding ongoing treatment, our support ensures people get the care they need, regardless of their financial circumstances.

Recreational activities. Recovery is about more than physical illness — it also means nurturing the spirit and fostering a sense of fulfilment and resilience. That's why we organise recreational activities and events, from art therapy sessions to sports tournaments, to lift spirits and support emotional wellbeing within Gaza's communities.

Why it matters

Your contribution has a direct impact on people's lives — whether it's a child receiving asthma medicine or an elderly person getting vital treatment. By supporting our medical aid work, you're not just providing relief in a crisis; you're offering hope, dignity, and the chance of a better future for Gaza's residents. Give today and be part of Gaza's healing process. Your generosity has the power to save lives, ease suffering, and bring comfort to those in need.
