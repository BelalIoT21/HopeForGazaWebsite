Empowering Minds: Education for All

Unlocking potential, igniting dreams — educational aid.

Widespread poverty and limited resources put enormous strain on Gaza's schools. Even so, education remains one of the most powerful catalysts for change and a genuine sign of hope:

School supplies. We provide essential school kits, textbooks, and stationery so that every child has the tools they need to learn and succeed — creating an environment suited to effective teaching and learning.

Support for teachers. We believe in the vital role teachers play in shaping the future, which is why we offer training and capacity-building programmes to help educators strengthen their skills and adopt effective teaching methods.

Access to education. A child's right to education should never be limited by financial hardship. That's why we offer scholarships and support programmes for children and young adults, ensuring no one is left behind because of financial difficulty, and helping people break the cycle of poverty.

Why it matters

Education prevents cycles of poverty, builds resilience, and opens doors to better futures. Investing in educational aid gives students and schools immediate relief while laying the groundwork for long-term change. Every child educated today becomes a catalyst for positive change tomorrow, creating a ripple effect of progress and opportunity throughout Gaza. You can make a difference by supporting a child's education or donating to our educational programmes — together, we can empower minds, lift communities, and create a brighter future for Gaza.
